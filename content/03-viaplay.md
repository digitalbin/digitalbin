# Viaplay

## 2020 - 2024

In a nutshell my team and I were responsible for the logged out pages with a mission to attract new customers.

Practically this meant a huge variety of tasks but the core of our mission was building the new web app for all logged out pages.

This included all existing markets as well as new expansions including but not limited to: United States, Poland, the Baltic states and United Kingdom.
